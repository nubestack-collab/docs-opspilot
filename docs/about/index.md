# About

The commercial and legal facts about OpsPilot: what it costs, what is in each release,
how to get help, and the terms you are using it under. The current version is
**0.5.1**; **Settings → About** shows the version you are running. OpsPilot is
proprietary software of NubeStack, licensed and not sold.

## Pages in this section

- **[Plans & subscription](plans.md)** — the 15-day trial and its three limits (10
  sessions open at once, your first 10 saved connections, AI on 2 connections you
  choose), what keeps working without a subscription, the price, and what the
  subscription includes. Read the note on AI inference costs before you budget a
  rollout.
- **[Release notes](release-notes.md)** — what changed in each release, newest first,
  with the known limitations of the current one.
- **[Support](support.md)** — how to reach NubeStack, the checks worth making first,
  what to include in a report, and how to report a security vulnerability.
- **[Privacy & licensing](licensing.md)** — no telemetry, what the optional license
  server receives, how licenses work, the legal notice and the third-party components
  OpsPilot bundles.

For buying, activation and licensing many computers, see the
[Licensing](../licensing/index.md) section.
