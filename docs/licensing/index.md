# Licensing

OpsPilot starts a 15-day free trial the first time it runs, with no account, email or
activation. After the trial the terminal keeps working with limits, and a subscription
lifts them. This section explains the limits, how to subscribe, how to activate
OpsPilot, and how IT teams license many computers.

## How it works

- **Licensed per user.** A subscription has one seat per person. Each seat has a license
  key that works on up to 2 of that person's devices.
- **Checked on your computer.** Licenses are signed by NubeStack and checked locally, so
  they work without a network connection.
- **Three ways to activate.** Online with the license key; offline, by carrying a request
  code out and a license file in; or with one organisation-wide deployment license that
  IT installs. Only online activation contacts NubeStack's license server.
- **No telemetry.** Licensing sends nothing about your work. The trial, offline
  activation and deployment licenses make no network connection at all.
- **The terminal always works.** Licensing limits AI, how many sessions are open at once
  and which saved connections open. It never closes a session.

## The plans at a glance

| | Free trial | Without a subscription | Subscription |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free for 15 days | Free | From $5 per user per month, or $50 per user per year |
| Sessions open at once | Up to 10 | Up to 10 | No limit |
| Saved connections you can open | Your first 10 | Your first 10 | All |
| AI | On 2 connections at a time, which you choose | Off | On every connection |
| AI Assistants and remote operation | Yes | No | Yes |
| Devices | This device | This device | Up to 2 per user |

Lower per-user prices apply from 5 seats. Current prices are at
<https://subscription.nubestack.com/opspilot>.
